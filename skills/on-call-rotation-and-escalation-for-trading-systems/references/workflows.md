# Workflows for On-Call Rotation and Escalation for Trading Systems

1. **Roster Registration**:
   - Register Primary, Secondary, and Executive engineers.
2. **Incident Creation & Escalation Monitoring**:
   - Track elapsed time since incident creation.
   - Escalate unacknowledged SEV-1 incidents from Primary to Secondary (3 mins) to Executive (5 mins).
3. **Acknowledgment Handling**:
   - Record incident acknowledgment and update state.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured escalation report.
