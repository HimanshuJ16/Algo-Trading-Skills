# Standards for On-Call Rotation and Escalation for Trading Systems

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| SEV-1 Response SLA | Maximum $\le 5$ minutes before Executive escalation. |
| SEV-2 Response SLA | Maximum $\le 15$ minutes before Secondary escalation. |
| Secondary Escalation Threshold (SEV-1) | 3 minutes without acknowledgment. |
