# Workflows for Section 1256 Contract Tax Treatment US Futures

1. **Eligibility Filtering**:
   - Filter trades for qualifying Section 1256 instruments (futures, SPX/NDX options).
2. **Dec 31 Mark-to-Market Valuation**:
   - Mark open position values to market as of the final business day of the tax year.
3. **Statutory 60/40 Split Calculation**:
   - Allocate 60% of net PnL to Long-Term and 40% to Short-Term.
4. **Form 6781 Summary Generation**:
   - Output structured summary for IRS Form 6781 filing.
