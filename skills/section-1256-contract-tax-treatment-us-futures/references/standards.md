# Standards for Section 1256 Contract Tax Treatment US Futures

| Parameter | IRS Standard Rule |
|---|---|
| Statutory Split | 60% Long-Term Capital Gains / 40% Short-Term Capital Gains. |
| Year-End MTM | All open Section 1256 contracts MUST be marked-to-market on Dec 31. |
| Tax Form | IRS Form 6781 Part I. |
