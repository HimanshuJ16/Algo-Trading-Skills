# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are traded instruments screened to confirm Section 1256 eligibility (futures, broad index options)?
- [ ] Are open contracts on Dec 31 marked-to-market at fair market value?
- [ ] Is 60% of net PnL allocated to Long-Term and 40% to Short-Term capital gains?
- [ ] Is tax summary prepared for filing IRS Form 6781 Part I?
