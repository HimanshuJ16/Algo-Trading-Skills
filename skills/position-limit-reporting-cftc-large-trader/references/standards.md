# Standards for Position Limit Reporting CFTC Large Trader

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Aggregation Level | Positions MUST be aggregated across all accounts under common ownership/control. |
| Form 102A Trigger | Mandatory filing when position $\ge$ CFTC contract reporting threshold. |
| Speculative Limit Enforcement | Breach of Part 150 Federal limits MUST trigger immediate compliance alert. |
