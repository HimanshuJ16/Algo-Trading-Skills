# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Is pin distance percentage calculated ($|Spot - Strike| / Spot * 100%$)?
- [ ] Are short options near the strike flagged for automated close prior to expiry?
- [ ] Is potential assigned share notional exposure ($|Q| * 100 * Spot$) calculated?
- [ ] Are contrary exercise deadlines (4:30 PM EST) strictly enforced?
