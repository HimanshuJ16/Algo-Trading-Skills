# Standards for Options Pin Risk Management at Expiry

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Pin Proximity Threshold | Distance $|S - K| / S \le 1.0\%$ on expiration day. |
| Cutoff Window | Positions MUST be audited and closed $\ge 1.0-2.0$ hours before market close. |
| Contrary Exercise Deadline | Post-market contrary instructions MUST be submitted before broker cutoff (4:30 PM EST). |
