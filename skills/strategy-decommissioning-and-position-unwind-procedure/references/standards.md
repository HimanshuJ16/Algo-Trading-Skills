# Standards for Strategy Decommissioning & Position Unwind Procedure

| Constraint | Standard Requirement |
|---|---|
| Entry Block | ALL new entry signals MUST be hard-blocked immediately upon decommissioning initiation. |
| Max ADV Slice | Individual liquidation slices MUST NOT exceed $10\%$ of ADV. |
| Treasury Return | Capital MUST be returned to fund treasury only after 100% position liquidation. |
