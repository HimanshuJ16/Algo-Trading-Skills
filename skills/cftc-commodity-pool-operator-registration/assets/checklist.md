# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Does the compliance engine evaluate the exemption criteria *pre-trade* rather than post-trade?
- [ ] Is the liquidation value updated dynamically to include unrealized PnL?
- [ ] Does the engine correctly allow a trade if *only one* of the two tests (5% margin OR 100% notional) is passed?
- [ ] Are equities and other non-commodity interests correctly excluded from the margin/notional numerators?
