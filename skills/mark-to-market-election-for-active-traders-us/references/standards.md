# Standards for Section 475(f) MTM Tax Accounting

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Wash Sale Exemption | Wash sale rules (IRC 1091) MUST NOT be applied to Section 475(f) MTM trading accounts. |
| Loss Deduction Limit | Net MTM trading losses MUST be fully deductible without $3,000 capital loss limits. |
| Tax Form Mapping | Section 475(f) MTM gains/losses MUST be reported on Form 4797 Part II (Ordinary Income). |
