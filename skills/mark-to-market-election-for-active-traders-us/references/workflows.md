# Workflows for Section 475(f) MTM Tax Accounting

1. **MTM Election Status Audit**:
   - Check if taxpayer has a valid IRS Section 475(f) election.
2. **Realized & Unrealized P&L Calculation**:
   - Compute realized trading P&L and mark open year-end positions to FMV.
3. **Wash Sale Exemption & Loss Cap Rules**:
   - Waive wash sale disallowance and remove the $3,000 capital loss cap for MTM traders.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured MTM tax report with Form 4797 Part II mapping.
