# Pre-Flight Checklist — IRC Section 475(f) MTM

US federal only. Not tax advice.

## Election is real
- [ ] Rev. Proc. 99-17 statement filed by the **unextended** due date of the
      **prior** year's return (new taxpayer: in books and records within
      2 months 15 days of the election year's first day)?
- [ ] Statement names the first effective year and the trade or business?
- [ ] Form 3115 filed for the year of change, with the § 481(a) adjustment?
- [ ] Tax year being computed is **not earlier** than the first effective year?
- [ ] For futures/broad-based index options: a **separate** § 475(f)(2)
      commodities election, and its 60/40 forfeiture, accepted deliberately?

## Computation
- [ ] Every open lot carried across a prior year end marked from its **prior
      year-end mark**, not its purchase price (§ 475(a))?
- [ ] Sale basis for previously marked lots set to the prior mark, not cost?
- [ ] § 1091 disallowance set to $0 for elected securities **only**?
- [ ] § 1092 straddle deferral still applied (§ 475(d)(1) preserves it)?
- [ ] Securities identified for investment on the **day acquired** excluded from
      the mark, kept capital, and left subject to § 1091?
- [ ] § 1256 contracts routed to Form 6781 unless the § 475(f)(2) election is in
      effect?

## Loss deductibility
- [ ] § 461(l) tested on **aggregate** business income, not trading alone?
- [ ] Threshold taken from a published amount for that tax year, not
      extrapolated?
- [ ] Disallowed excess carried forward as an NOL (80% limited on use)?
- [ ] Non-elected branch: $1,500 used for married filing separately?
- [ ] Non-elected branch: § 1212(b) capital loss carryforward reported, not
      discarded?

## Output
- [ ] Elected P&L mapped to **Form 4797 Part II**, never Schedule D?
- [ ] Self-employment tax **not** applied to trading gains?
- [ ] Every routed-out, capped or unevaluated item surfaced in the report rather
      than silently dropped?
- [ ] Results reviewed by a qualified US tax professional before filing?
