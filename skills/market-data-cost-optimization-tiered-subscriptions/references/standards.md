# Standards for Market Data Cost Optimization

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Active Position Tier | Active position symbols MUST be subscribed to Tier 1 or Tier 2 feeds. |
| Inactive Demotion Rule | Symbols with no position and > 30 days inactivity MUST be demoted to Tier 3 EOD. |
| Spend Audit Frequency | Market data feed entitlements MUST be audited monthly. |
