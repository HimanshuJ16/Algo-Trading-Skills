# Workflows for Market Data Cost Optimization

1. **Symbol Activity Classification**:
   - Audit open position status, signal state, and trade recency for each symbol.
2. **Tier Allocation & Promotion/Demotion**:
   - Assign TIER1, TIER2, or TIER3 based on activity rules.
3. **Spend & Savings Audit**:
   - Calculate baseline vs optimized spend and compute total cost savings.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured market data cost report.
