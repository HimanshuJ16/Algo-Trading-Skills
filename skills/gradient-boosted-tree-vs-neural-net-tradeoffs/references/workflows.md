# Workflows for Model Family Trade-Off Selection

1. **Dataset & Constraint Ingestion**:
   - Ingest data modality, sample size, latency budget, and regulatory compliance rules.
2. **5-Dimension Trade-Off Scoring**:
   - Score GBDT vs Neural Net across Tabular, Sequential, Latency, Compliance, and Regime Shift dimensions.
3. **Model Family Decision**:
   - Recommend LightGBM/XGBoost, Deep Learning (LSTM/Transformer), or Hybrid Ensemble.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Generate detailed trade-off report for Model Risk Committee.
