# Standards for Post-Mortem Culture and Blameless Review Process

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Blameless Language Policy | Accusatory terms MUST be eliminated and reframed into systemic factors. |
| Systemic Factor Requirement | Every post-mortem MUST identify at least 2 architectural/process factors. |
| Review SLA | Blameless review meeting MUST occur within $5$ business days of incident recovery. |
