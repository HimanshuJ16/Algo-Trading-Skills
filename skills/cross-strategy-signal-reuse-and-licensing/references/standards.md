# Standards for Cross-Strategy Signal Reuse and Licensing

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| AUM Capacity Cap | Total subscribed AUM across strategy pods MUST NOT exceed signal `max_aum_capacity_usd`. |
| Arm's Length Fee Attribution | Internal signal licensing MUST adhere to OECD arm's-length transfer pricing rules. |
| Entitlement Permissioning | Signal data feeds MUST strictly block un-licensed strategy pod IDs. |
