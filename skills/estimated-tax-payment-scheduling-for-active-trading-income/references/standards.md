# Standards for Estimated Tax Payment Scheduling

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| High-Income Safe Harbor | High-income traders ($\text{AGI} > \$150,000$) MUST pay $110\%$ of prior year tax to qualify for safe harbor. |
| Quarterly Deadlines | Estimated tax payments MUST be scheduled on April 15, June 15, Sept 15, Jan 15. |
| Reserve Isolation | Tax reserves MUST be isolated from trading margin to avoid forced liquidations. |
