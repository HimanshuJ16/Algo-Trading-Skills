# Workflows for Estimated Tax Payment Scheduling

1. **Safe Harbor Target Calculation**:
   - Evaluate prior year AGI threshold (100% vs 110%) and current year 90% target.
2. **Quarterly Installment Allocation**:
   - Divide annual safe harbor into 4 quarterly installments (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4).
3. **Trading Capital Reserve Lockup**:
   - Set aside required quarterly tax amounts in liquid risk-free reserves.
4. **Schedule Generation & Monitoring**:
   - Track payments made and monitor upcoming IRS due dates.
