# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Is live Information Ratio audited against the $\ge 0.50$ minimum threshold?
- [ ] Is live max drawdown capped at $\le 1.5\times$ historical backtest max drawdown?
- [ ] Is Information Coefficient (IC) t-statistic monitored for $\ge 1.96$ statistical significance?
- [ ] Is mandatory retirement triggered automatically when $\ge 3$ quantitative criteria fail?
