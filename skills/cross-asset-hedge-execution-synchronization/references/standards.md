# Standards for Cross-Asset Hedge Execution Synchronization

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Max Synchronization SLA | Hedge leg orders MUST be dispatched within `max_sync_delay_ms` (default 100 ms) of the primary fill notification; the engine measures dispatch latency (`mark_dispatched`) and fill latency separately. The default is an engineering default, not a regulatory or venue requirement — calibrate per venue, instrument, and strategy. |
| Partial Fill Hedging | Partial fills MUST be hedged incrementally without waiting for full order completion — on both legs: one hedge order per primary fill event, and hedge fill callbacks accumulated until the cumulative fill reaches the target. |
| Emergency Unwind Threshold | Hedges still incomplete after `unhedged_timeout_ms` (default 500 ms) MUST trigger emergency unwinding of the primary leg via the timeout sweep (`enforce_unhedged_timeouts`), driven by a timer rather than the arrival of a late fill. Like the SLA above, the 500 ms figure is a configurable engineering default. |
| Side & Quantity Validation | Wrong-side hedge fills, non-finite/zero quantities, hedge quantities that round to zero, and fills timestamped before the primary fill MUST be rejected before they mutate hedge state. |
| Fill Idempotency | `fill_id` MUST be the deduplication key for hedge generation. A redelivered primary fill MUST NOT produce a second hedge order, and MUST NOT reset the accumulated fill state of a live hedge. Duplicate detection is an application-layer obligation under FIX and a pre-trade control obligation under SEC Rule 15c3-5(c)(1)(ii). |
| Single Unwind Path | A breach of `unhedged_timeout_ms` MUST route to the emergency unwind handler exactly once, whether it is first observed by the timer sweep or by a late fill callback. The two paths MUST NOT differ in whether the primary leg is unwound. |
| State Synchronization | If the timeout sweep and fill callbacks run on different threads, hedge state MUST be synchronized, and the unwind handler MUST be invoked outside that synchronization so third-party handler code cannot stall or deadlock fill processing. |

## External References (verified 2026-08)

| Source | Reference | Relevance | Jurisdiction |
|---|---|---|---|
| SEC | Rule 15c3-5 (Market Access Rule), adopted 2010 — [staff compliance guide (34-63241)](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/final/2010/34-63241-secg.htm) | Automated order flow, including hedge orders, is subject to pre-trade risk controls (credit/capital thresholds, erroneous-order and duplicate-order prevention) that must run before orders reach the market — budget their latency inside the synchronization SLA | US |
| ESMA / EUR-Lex | MiFID II Article 17 (algorithmic trading) and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/589 ("RTS 6"): [Article 12 — Kill functionality](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2017/589/oj/eng) (immediate emergency cancellation of unexecuted orders, reproduced in the [FCA Handbook](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/technical-standards/s119c1039)); Article 15 — pre-trade controls on order entry (price collars, maximum order values/volumes, message limits) | The emergency-unwind path maps to the required kill capability; pre-trade control article bounds what checks may run on the hedge dispatch path | EU |
| SEBI | Circular CIR/MRD/DP/09/2012, [Broad Guidelines on Algorithmic Trading](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/mar-2012/broad-guidelines-on-algorithmic-trading_22471.html) | Exchange-enforced algo risk controls (order/price-level checks) that automated hedge order flow must satisfy in India | India |
| OIC (OCC education) | [Options Basics](https://www.optionseducation.org/optionsoverview/options-basics) — "Equity option contracts usually represent 100 shares of the underlying stock" | Contract multiplier that MUST be folded into `hedge_ratio` (0.50 delta × 100 = 50 shares/contract); adjusted contracts can deviate | US |

| FIX Trading Community | FIX session layer: `PossDupFlag` (tag 43) and `PossResend` (tag 97). | Hull, J. C. (2017) | "Optimal delta hedging for options", Journal of Banking & Finance | Canonical treatment of delta-hedge ratio construction and why the hedge must be rebalanced as delta moves | Academic |

Note: the venue behaviour above is US-options-specific and varies by exchange and order type — verify that your particular combo executes as a package before relying on it. The 100 ms dispatch SLA and 500 ms unwind timeout are engineering defaults of this engine, not legal or venue requirements. Nothing in the sources above mandates a specific latency figure; regulators require effective controls (pre-trade limits, kill capability), while specific latency budgets are strategy- and venue-specific design choices.
