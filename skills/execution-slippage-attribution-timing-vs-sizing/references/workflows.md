# Workflows for Execution Slippage Attribution

1. **Price Data Ingestion**:
   - Ingest decision price, arrival price, and average fill price.
2. **Shortfall Decomposition**:
   - Calculate total IS, timing delay IS, and sizing impact IS.
3. **Primary Driver Identification**:
   - Identify whether timing or sizing contributes most to total cost.
4. **Strategy Action Feedback**:
   - Issue recommendations to adjust dispatch speed or participation ceilings.
