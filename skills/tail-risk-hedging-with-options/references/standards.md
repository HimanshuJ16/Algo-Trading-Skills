# Risk Standards for Options Tail Risk Hedging

## Key Sizing & Budgeting Standards

- **Maximum Annual Premium Drag**: Cap at 1.5%–3.0% of portfolio AUM per year.
- **Strike Moneyness**: Target 10% to 20% out-of-the-money put options ($K = 0.80 \times S$ to $0.90 \times S$).
- **Expiration Horizon**: Target 60 to 90 days to expiration (DTE) to optimize vega sensitivity vs theta decay.
- **Roll Schedule**: Roll contracts at 30 DTE to avoid exponential theta acceleration.
