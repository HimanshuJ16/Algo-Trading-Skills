# Standards for Implementation Shortfall Minimization

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Benchmark Price | Implementation Shortfall MUST be benchmarked against Decision Price ($P_0$). |
| Cost Decomposition | Total IS MUST decompose into Impact Cost, Opportunity Cost, and Explicit Fees. |
| Measurement Unit | Total Implementation Shortfall MUST be reported in both total USD and basis points (bps). |
