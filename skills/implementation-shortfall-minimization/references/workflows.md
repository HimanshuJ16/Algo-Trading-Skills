# Workflows for Implementation Shortfall Minimization

1. **Almgren-Chriss Trajectory Calculation**:
   - Compute optimal trading schedule balancing market impact vs volatility risk.
2. **Trade Fill Ingestion & Benchmark Comparison**:
   - Ingest executed trade fills and compare against Decision Price ($P_0$).
3. **IS Cost Decomposition**:
   - Calculate Impact Cost, Opportunity Cost, and Explicit Fees in USD and bps.
4. **Post-Trade TCA Reporting**:
   - Output structured IS execution report.
