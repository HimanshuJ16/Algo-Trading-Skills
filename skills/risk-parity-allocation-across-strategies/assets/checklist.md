# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are annualized volatilities estimated for all portfolio strategies?
- [ ] Are capital weights calculated using inverse-volatility ($1/\sigma_i$) or full ERC optimization?
- [ ] Is Marginal Contribution to Risk (MCR) decomposed considering strategy covariance?
- [ ] Is risk parity contribution error verified to be $\le 5\%$ before capital deployment?
