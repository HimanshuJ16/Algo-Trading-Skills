# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are Days to Liquidate (DTL) calculated per symbol at $10\%$ participation cap?
- [ ] Are bid-ask spread spikes ($> 2.0x$ normal) flagged in real time?
- [ ] Are order book depth drops ($> 50\%$) monitored across top L2 levels?
- [ ] Is Liquidity-Adjusted VaR (L-VaR) updated for portfolio risk governance?
