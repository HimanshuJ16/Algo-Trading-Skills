# Standards for Tax Classification

| Condition | Asset Class | Hold Period | Output Classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Intraday (No Delivery) | Equity | $< 1$ Day | Speculative Business Income |
| High Frequency/Algo | Equity | $1$ Day to $< 365$ Days | Non-Speculative Business Income (usually) |
| Derivatives (F&O) | Derivative | Any | Non-Speculative Business Income |
| Investment Hold | Equity | $\ge 365$ Days | Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) |

*Note: These standards are modeled on a typical generalized framework (e.g., India Income Tax / Canadian CRA general practices). Always consult a local tax professional.*
