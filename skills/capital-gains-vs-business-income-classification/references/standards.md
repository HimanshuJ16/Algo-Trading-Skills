# Standards for Capital Gains vs Business Income Classification

There is no cross-border taxonomy for this question. The categories themselves differ,
so each jurisdiction is stated separately. Nothing below is tax advice, and none of it
substitutes for an adviser applying the facts of a particular taxpayer.

## India — Income-tax Act, 1961

| Condition | Classification | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Contract for purchase or sale of stocks and shares "periodically or ultimately settled otherwise than by the actual delivery or transfer" of the scrip | Speculative business income | s.43(5), main limb |
| Eligible transaction in trading in derivatives carried out on a **recognised stock exchange** | Excluded from "speculative transaction" — non-speculative business income | s.43(5) proviso (d), inserted w.e.f. 1 April 2006 |
| Derivative **not** on a recognised stock exchange | Falls back to the main limb — speculative | s.43(5) |
| Listed security held for **not more than 12 months** before transfer | Short-term capital asset | s.2(42A) |
| Any other capital asset held for **not more than 24 months** | Short-term capital asset | s.2(42A), as amended by the Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024, w.e.f. 23 July 2024 |
| Listed shares held more than 12 months, where the taxpayer opts for capital-gains treatment | Capital gains — the Assessing Officer is not to dispute it, but the stand binds subsequent assessment years | CBDT Circular No. 6/2016, dated 29 February 2016 |
| Shares the taxpayer itself treats as stock-in-trade, irrespective of holding period | Business income | CBDT Circular No. 6/2016 |
| Speculative business loss | Set off only against speculative business income | s.73 |

The Finance (No. 2) Act, 2024 also reset the rates for transfers on or after 23 July 2024
(s.111A short-term at 20%, s.112A long-term at 12.5% above a ₹1.25 lakh annual threshold).
This skill does **not** apply rates — it only produces the category a rate is later applied to.

## United States — Internal Revenue Code

| Condition | Classification | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Capital asset held for **more than one year** | Long-term capital gain or loss | IRC s.1222; IRS Topic 409 |
| Capital asset held for **one year or less** | Short-term capital gain or loss | IRC s.1222; IRS Topic 409 |
| Holding-period counting | "Count from the day after the day you acquired the asset up to and including the day you disposed of the asset" | IRS Topic 409 |
| Trader in securities **without** a s.475(f) election | Gains and losses remain **capital** — Schedule D / Form 8949 | IRS Topic 429 |
| Trader in securities **with** a timely, valid s.475(f) mark-to-market election | Gains and losses are **ordinary** — Form 4797 | IRC s.475(f); IRS Topic 429 |
| Regulated futures, broad-based index options, certain foreign currency contracts, dealer equity options | s.1256 contracts — statutory 60/40 split on Form 6781 | IRC s.1256 |

There is **no speculative-business category** in US federal tax law. Day-trading frequency
alone changes nothing about the capital/ordinary character of a gain; only the election does.
The interaction between a s.475(f) securities election and s.1256 contracts is out of scope
here — see `section-1256-contract-tax-treatment-us-futures`.

## Canada — Income Tax Act

| Condition | Classification | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Holding period | **Irrelevant.** Canada draws no long-term/short-term distinction; the same inclusion rate applies regardless of how long the property was held | ITA capital gains regime |
| Securities dispositions generally | Capital account or income account, decided on the facts (frequency, intention, financing, expertise, period of ownership) | IT-479R, *Transactions in Securities* |
| Election under ITA s.39(4), Form T123 | Every Canadian security the taxpayer owns in that and every later year is **deemed capital property**; the election cannot be rescinded | ITA s.39(4) |
| Availability of the s.39(4) election | **Not available** to traders or dealers in securities | ITA s.39(5) |
| Commodity futures held by a "speculator" | May be reported as capital gains and losses provided the practice is followed **consistently from year to year**; income treatment once adopted may not be changed | IT-346R, *Commodity Futures and Certain Commodities* |
