# Standards for Opportunity Cost Tracking for Idle Capital

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Benchmark Rate | SOFR (Secured Overnight Financing Rate) or US T-Bill rate. |
| Max Target Idle Ratio | Maximum $\le 5.0\%$ of total portfolio capital. |
| Min Sweep Threshold | Minimum $\$100,000$ USD idle cash to trigger sweep. |
