# Standards for Cross-Account Aggregate Risk View

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Firm-Wide GMV Cap | Aggregate Gross Market Value across all accounts MUST NOT exceed firm risk mandate (e.g. $10,000,000). |
| Internal Offsetting Alert | Concurrent long/short positions in the same asset across sub-accounts MUST be flagged for internal netting. |
| Consolidated Margin Limit | Total firm-wide margin utilization MUST NOT exceed 80% of aggregate margin capacity. |
