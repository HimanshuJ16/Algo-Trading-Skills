# Workflows for Market Data Entitlement Governance

1. **Subscriber Classification & Non-Display Audit**:
   - Audit professional subscriber status and non-display algorithmic licensing requirements.
2. **Venue Entitlement & Expiration Gate**:
   - Verify requested venue is licensed and check license expiry timestamps.
3. **Stream Authorization Decision**:
   - Grant or deny market data stream access based on compliance rules.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured entitlement audit report.
