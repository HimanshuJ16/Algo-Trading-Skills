# Standards for CIRO (IIROC) Compliance

| Control Element | CIRO Mandate (UMIR 7.1 / NI 23-103) |
|---|---|
| Timing | Must be **Pre-Trade** and Automated. |
| Erroneous Orders | Must prevent orders that exceed reasonable price/size thresholds (Fat-finger prevention). |
| Capital Limits | Must ensure aggregate open orders do not exceed firm capital limits. |
| Short Selling | Must correctly flag `Short` or `SME` (Short-Marking Exempt) prior to routing. |
