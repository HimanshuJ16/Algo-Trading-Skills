# Standards for Data Localization Requirements for Trade Records

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| China Data Residency | Chinese trade records MUST reside in China AWS (`cn-north-1`/`cn-northwest-1`). Egress is strictly prohibited. |
| India Payment & Trade Residency | Indian trade ledgers MUST reside on primary servers in India (`ap-south-1`). |
| SEC Rule 17a-4 Retention | US trade records MUST maintain 6-year WORM immutable retention. |
