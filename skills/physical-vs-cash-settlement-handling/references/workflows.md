# Workflows for Physical vs Cash Settlement Handling

1. **Settlement Type Classification**:
   - Classify contract as CASH or PHYSICAL settlement.
2. **Cash Settlement Expiration**:
   - Compute net cash PnL credit/debit at expiration.
3. **Physical Delivery Audit**:
   - Calculate full notional delivery requirement and audit FND proximity.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured settlement report.
