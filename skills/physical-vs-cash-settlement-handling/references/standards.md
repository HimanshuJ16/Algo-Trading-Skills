# Standards for Physical vs Cash Settlement Handling

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| First Notice Date Cutoff | MANDATORY liquidation/roll $\ge 3$ days prior to FND for non-delivering accounts. |
| Physical Notional Formula | $V_{\text{delivery}} = |Q| \cdot \text{Multiplier} \cdot P_{\text{settle}}$. |
| Cash Settlement | $100\%$ automated PnL cash credit/debit at expiration. |
