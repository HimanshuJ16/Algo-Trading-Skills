# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are bid-ask spread and top-of-book depth monitored for expiring futures?
- [ ] Are market orders blocked when spread exceeds 2.0 ticks?
- [ ] Is 50% position size haircut applied when depth drops $< 30\%$ baseline?
- [ ] Are new entry trades blocked and rolls mandated when $DBE \le 2$ days?
