# Standards for Strategy Committee Governance for Capital Allocation Decisions

| Control Requirement | Mandatory Standard |
|---|---|
| Quorum Threshold | Minimum 50% of voting committee members MUST be present. |
| CRO Veto Power | Chief Risk Officer (CRO) holds absolute veto power over risk limit breaches. |
| Strategy Concentration Cap | Maximum 20% of fund total AUM per individual strategy. |
