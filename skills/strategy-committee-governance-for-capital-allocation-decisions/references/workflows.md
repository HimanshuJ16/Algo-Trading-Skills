# Workflows for Strategy Committee Governance for Capital Allocation Decisions

1. **Proposal Submission**:
   - Submit allocation proposal specifying current and target AUM.
2. **Risk Limit Pre-Screen**:
   - Audit proposed allocation against single strategy concentration cap (e.g. 20% AUM).
3. **Quorum & Vote Collection**:
   - Verify quorum ($\ge 50\%$) and collect member votes with rationales.
4. **Veto Audit & Decision Output**:
   - Evaluate CRO veto power and majority vote; output governance decision.
