# Workflows for Strategy Research-to-Production Pipeline Governance

1. **Gatekeeping Request Submission**:
   - Collect artifacts (Git commit, dataset checksum, backtest Sharpe, paper trading error).
2. **Reproducibility & Backtest Verification**:
   - Audit code versioning and backtest quantitative thresholds.
3. **Shadow Execution & Risk Sign-off**:
   - Verify 14-day shadow paper trading and Risk Committee sign-off.
4. **Audit Ledger Logging**:
   - Log SHA-256 audit hash and execute stage promotion.
