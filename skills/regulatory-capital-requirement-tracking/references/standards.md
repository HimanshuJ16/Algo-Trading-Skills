# Standards for Regulatory Capital Requirement Tracking

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Net Capital Rule (SEC 15c3-1) | Net liquid capital MUST exceed statutory base minimum + variable haircut additions at all times. |
| Early Warning Buffer | System MUST trigger warning alerts when Net Capital falls below $120\%$ of minimum requirement. |
| Valuation Frequency | Regulatory capital adequacy MUST be calculated at least daily at end-of-day. |
