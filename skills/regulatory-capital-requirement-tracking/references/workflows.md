# Workflows for Regulatory Capital Requirement Tracking

1. **Net Capital Calculation**:
   - Subtract illiquid deductions and total liabilities from liquid assets plus qualified subordinated debt.
2. **Total Requirement Evaluation**:
   - Add variable risk-weighted requirement to base statutory minimum capital.
3. **Headroom & Warning Buffer Check**:
   - Compare net capital against total required and early warning buffer (120%).
4. **Report & Alert Generation**:
   - Output structured capital status report and trigger alerts on warning/breach states.
