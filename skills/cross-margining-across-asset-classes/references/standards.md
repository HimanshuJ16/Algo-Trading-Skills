# Standards for Cross-Margining Across Asset Classes

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Minimum Margin Floor | Cross-margin calculations MUST enforce a minimum risk floor of $20\%$ of standalone margin sum. |
| Clearing House Approval | Cross-margin offsets MUST only apply to asset pairs covered by active CME-OCC or CME-FICC agreements. |
| Real-Time Offset Calculation | Cross-margin savings MUST be recalculated in real-time upon order fills or market price movements. |
