# Standards for Post-Collapse Exchange Risk Framework

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Proof of Reserves Coverage | Merkle-tree PoR ratio MUST be $\ge 100\%$ of client liabilities. |
| Native Token Cap | Native exchange token collateral MUST NOT exceed $5.0\%$ of total margin. |
| Single Exchange NAV Cap | Capital deployed on a single exchange MUST NOT exceed $20.0\%$ of fund NAV. |
