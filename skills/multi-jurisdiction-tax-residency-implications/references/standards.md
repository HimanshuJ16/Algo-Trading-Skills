# Standards for Multi-Jurisdiction Tax Residency

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Physical Presence Threshold | 183 days per calendar/tax year triggers statutory tax residency. |
| Foreign Tax Credit Limit | $\text{FTC} = \min(\text{Tax}_{\text{foreign}}, \text{Tax}_{\text{domestic}})$. |
| POEM Override | POEM location overrides shell incorporation jurisdiction under OECD DTAA rules. |
