# Workflows for Multi-Jurisdiction Tax Residency

1. **Substantial Presence & POEM Audit**:
   - Audit 183-day physical presence and Place of Effective Management (POEM).
2. **DTAA WHT & FTC Relief Calculation**:
   - Apply treaty withholding tax rates and calculate Foreign Tax Credit (FTC) offsets.
3. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured tax residency report.
