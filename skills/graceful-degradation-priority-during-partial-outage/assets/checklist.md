# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are task priorities categorized (P1 Risk/Cancel, P2 Exits, P3 Entries, P4 Analytics)?
- [ ] Is system health (CPU, packet loss, latency) continuously monitored?
- [ ] Are P4 analytics tasks automatically shed during partial degradation?
- [ ] Is Capital Preservation Mode (P1 only) activated during critical outages?
