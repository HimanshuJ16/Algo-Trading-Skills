# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Is ISO 4217 priority hierarchy configured for all traded currencies?
- [ ] Are non-standard/inverted quotes (e.g. `USD/EUR`) automatically identified and flipped?
- [ ] Are bid and ask prices cross-inverted ($\text{Bid}_{\text{std}} = 1/\text{Ask}_{\text{inv}}$)?
- [ ] Are pip sizes and spread pips accurately calculated for JPY vs non-JPY pairs?
