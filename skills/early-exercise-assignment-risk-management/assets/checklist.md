# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are short call positions audited against upcoming ex-dividend dates?
- [ ] Is extrinsic value ($\text{Market Price} - \text{Intrinsic}$) calculated for all short option positions?
- [ ] Are short calls closed or rolled if $D_{\text{usd}} > \text{Extrinsic Value}$?
- [ ] Are American-style options distinguished from European-style options?
