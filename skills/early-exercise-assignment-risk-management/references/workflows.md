# Workflows for Early Exercise Assignment Risk Management

1. **Intrinsic & Extrinsic Decomposition**:
   - Compute option intrinsic and extrinsic time value.
2. **Ex-Dividend Capture Audit**:
   - Compare upcoming dividend against remaining extrinsic value.
3. **Assignment Risk Scoring**:
   - Classify risk (`CRITICAL_ASSIGNMENT_RISK`, `HIGH_ASSIGNMENT_RISK`, `LOW_RISK`).
4. **Roll & Close Dispatch**:
   - Issue position close/roll directives before ex-dividend cutoff.
