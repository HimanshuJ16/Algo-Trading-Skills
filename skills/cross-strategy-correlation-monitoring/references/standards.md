# Standards for Cross-Strategy Correlation Monitoring

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Pairwise Correlation Threshold | Pairwise strategy PnL correlation $\rho \ge 0.70$ MUST trigger risk alerts. |
| Redundancy Threshold | Pairwise correlation $\rho \ge 0.85$ MUST trigger mandatory pod capital re-allocation review. |
| Minimum Diversification Ratio | Portfolio Diversification Ratio ($DR$) MUST remain above $1.20$. |
