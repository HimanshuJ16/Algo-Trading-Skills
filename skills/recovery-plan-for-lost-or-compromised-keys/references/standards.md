# Standards for Recovery Plan for Lost or Compromised Keys

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Recovery Drill Cadence | Full key recovery drill MUST be conducted every $90$ days. |
| Shamir Shard Surplus | Verified shards MUST exceed threshold by at least $1$ shard. |
| Emergency Sweep Wallet | Pre-configured sweep wallet MUST be ready for immediate fund evacuation. |
