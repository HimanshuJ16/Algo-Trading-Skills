# Workflows for Options Chain Expiry Cycle Conventions by Exchange

1. **3rd Friday Monthly Expiry Calculation**:
   - Compute 3rd Friday date for target year and month.
2. **Settlement & Exercise Style Resolution**:
   - Assign AM vs PM settlement (SOQ vs Closing) and European vs American exercise style.
3. **DTE Calculation**:
   - Compute Days to Expiration (DTE) relative to reference date.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured options convention report.
