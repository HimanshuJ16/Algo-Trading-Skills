# Pre-Flight Checklist — Swiss algorithmic trading controls (SIX Swiss Exchange)

Provision references: FMIO (FinfraV, SR 958.11) Art. 31; SIX Trading Rules; SIX
Directives 3 and 7; FINMA Circular 2013/8. Verbatim text in `references/standards.md`.

## Scope

- [ ] Is the venue **SIX Swiss Exchange**? BX Swiss and SDX impose their own participant
      rules — BX's published Participant Rules contain no algorithmic-trading clause —
      so this control set does not transfer to them.
- [ ] Have you confirmed the duty chain: **FMIA is silent on algorithmic trading**; the
      provision is FMIO Art. 31, addressed to the *trading venue*, which "shall require
      participants" to comply through its rulebook?

## Identification and notification — SIX Trading Rules cl. 11.1.4 para. 1

- [ ] Are orders generated by algorithmic trading flagged, on **every** order path
      (Directive 3 cl. 5.1.3 lit. h no. 1 makes it a per-order attribute)?
- [ ] Does **each algorithm** carry its own separate identification — not one shared
      across parameter variants (lit. h no. 2, FMIO Art. 31 para. 1 lit. b)?
- [ ] Is the initiating trader indicated (lit. h no. 3, FMIO Art. 31 para. 1 lit. c)?
- [ ] Has the **operation of algorithmic trading been reported to the Exchange**? An
      internal inventory alone does not discharge this outward limb.

## Records — cl. 11.1.4 para. 2, FMIO Art. 31 para. 2

- [ ] Are sent orders kept on file **including cancellations**? These are the records a
      fills-only pipeline drops, and the ones an order-to-trade or Art. 143 FMIA enquiry
      needs.

## Risk controls — FMIO Art. 31 para. 2 lit. a–e / Directive 3 cl. 10 para. 1

- [ ] Are systems robust and capacity-tested for **peak** order and announcement
      volumes (lit. a)?
- [ ] Are trading thresholds and upper limits in place (lit. b) — **and** is their
      calibration basis recorded? The provision requires them to be *appropriate*, which
      cannot be demonstrated without the record.
- [ ] Are controls in place against **Arts. 142 and 143 FMIA** — insider trading, price
      and market manipulation (lit. d, FINMA Circ. 13/8 mn 62)?
- [ ] Are algorithms and control mechanisms subject to appropriate tests (lit. e)?
- [ ] Is there a configured limit on the proportion of unexecuted orders to transactions
      (lit. e no. 1)?
- [ ] Can the order flow be **slowed when system capacity is at risk**, with a rate
      actually configured (lit. e no. 2)?
- [ ] Is the minimum tick size limited and enforced (lit. e no. 3)?

## Supervisory documentation — FINMA Circular 2013/8 mn 63

- [ ] Are the **key features of the strategy documented so a third party can understand
      them**, and is a named accountable owner recorded?

## Sponsored access / DEA — only if you provide it

- [ ] Are you **authorised and able at any time to delete DEA client orders** on the
      Exchange's instruction (SIX Trading Rules cl. 4.3.4 para. 2)?
- [ ] Are the Exchange's mandatory pre-/at-trade controls for Sponsored User flow
      configured and monitored (Directive 7 cl. 8 para. 2)?
- [ ] Is the Directive 7 cl. 8 para. 4 **kill switch** over the Sponsored User's open
      orders and order transmission available and tested?
- [ ] Has the effectiveness of these controls been assessed and is it reviewed regularly
      (cl. 8 para. 5)?

## Claims to check you are *not* making

- [ ] No message-rate cap is presented as a Swiss requirement. There is none —
      lit. e no. 2 requires the *ability* to slow the flow, and states no rate.
- [ ] No price-collar percentage or notional ceiling is attributed to Swiss law.
      Lit. b says "appropriate ... thresholds and upper limits" and stops there.
- [ ] **Microsecond timestamps are not attributed to FINMA.** FinfraV-FINMA Art. 1
      para. 2 lit. b asks for the time of order receipt with no precision. Microsecond
      granularity is MiFID II RTS 25 — an EU instrument.
- [ ] A **general** mandatory kill switch is not booked as an FMIO Art. 31 obligation.
      The only one in the SIX rulebook is Directive 7 cl. 8 para. 4, scoped to
      Sponsored Access.
- [ ] No duty is cited as "FinfraG Art. 31" or attributed to the Act. The Act is silent.
- [ ] A partial readiness score is not reported as partial compliance.

## Reform watch

- [ ] Have you checked the current status of the FMIA revision? The EFD *FinfraG-Review*
      (30 September 2022) proposes **defining** algorithmic trading and high-frequency
      trading — neither is defined today — and tightening flagging toward unique and
      permanent algorithm identification on the EU model. Consultation ran 19 June to
      11 October 2024.
