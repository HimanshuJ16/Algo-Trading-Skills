# Workflows for ASIC Automated Order Processing

1. **System Initialization**: Load the `AsicMarketIntegrityConfig` with hard limits approved by the firm's compliance officer.
2. **Pre-Trade Hook**: Every order generated by the strategy engine must pass synchronously through `AsicAopPreTradeFilter.run_checks()` *before* being serialized to the FIX connection.
3. **Emergency Halt**: If the strategy goes rogue or the market flashes, the operations team triggers `AsicKillSwitchManager.trigger_kill_switch()`.
4. **Audit Logging**: All rejected orders are logged with their specific rejection reason to maintain the required ASIC audit trail.
