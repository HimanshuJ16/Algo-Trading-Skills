# Workflows for EU Market Abuse Regulation (MAR) Surveillance

1. **Order & Trade Log Ingestion**:
   - Ingest order creations, modifications, cancellations, and fills.
2. **Abuse Pattern Detection**:
   - Evaluate Wash Trading, Spoofing/Layering, and Quote Stuffing algorithms.
3. **STOR Regulatory Filing Generation**:
   - Format ESMA RTS 25 STOR report data for NCA submission.
4. **Compliance Alert Escalation**:
   - Escalate suspicious activities to Compliance Officers without delay.
