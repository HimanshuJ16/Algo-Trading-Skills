# Workflows for Custody Insurance Assessment

1. **Hot Wallet Crime Audit**:
   - Audit Crime policy limits against hot/warm wallet trading balances.
2. **Cold Specie & Pooled Dilution Audit**:
   - Calculate custodian pro-rata dilution factor ($\frac{\text{Specie Limit}}{\text{Custodian Total Cold AUM}}$).
3. **Net Shortfall Calculation**:
   - Compute total uninsured capital exposure in USD and percentage.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured custody insurance report.
