# Standards for Crypto Custody Insurance Assessment

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Hot Wallet Crime Ratio | Hot wallet Crime policy coverage SHOULD equal $100\%$ of active hot wallet AUM. |
| Pooled Specie Dilution | Cold vault coverage MUST account for custodian pooled client dilution ratios. |
| Shortfall Reporting | Net uninsured capital MUST be explicitly reported in USD and percentage. |
