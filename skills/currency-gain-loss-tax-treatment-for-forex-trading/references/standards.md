# Standards for Forex Currency Gain/Loss Tax Treatment

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Section 988 Default | Spot forex gains/losses MUST default to Section 988 ordinary income/loss unless an opt-out election is documented. |
| Section 1256 Split | Section 1256 opt-out MUST calculate taxes as 60% Long-Term Capital Gains and 40% Short-Term Capital Gains. |
| Contemporaneous Election Log | Section 988 opt-out elections MUST be timestamped prior to executing the first trade of the tax year. |
