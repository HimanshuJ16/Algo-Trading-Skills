# Standards for Annual Compliance Attestation

| Regulatory Body | Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **SEC** | Rule 206(4)-7 | Registered Investment Advisers must conduct an annual review of the adequacy of their written compliance policies. |
| **FINRA** | Rule 3130 | Broker-Dealer CEOs must certify annually that the firm has compliance processes in place, requiring a documented meeting with the CCO. |
| **SEC / FINRA Exams** | Quant Focus | Firms utilizing algorithmic strategies must specifically document risk controls, code integrity tests, and electronic trade surveillance. |
