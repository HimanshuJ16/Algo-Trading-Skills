# Standards for Annual Compliance Attestation

| Regulatory Body | Rule | Requirement | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| **SEC** | Rule 206(4)-7 | Registered Investment Advisers must conduct an annual REVIEW of the adequacy of their written compliance policies and designate a CCO. Not a standalone "CCO annual report." | Adviser annual-review records retained 5 years (first 2 in-office) per Rule 204-2(a)(16)(ii). |
| **SEC** | Rule 204-2(a)(16)(ii) | Record of the annual review of written policies/procedures. | 5 years (first 2 in an appropriate office of the adviser). |
| **FINRA** | Rule 3130 | BD CEOs must certify annually that the firm has compliance processes in place; requires a documented CEO-CCO meeting at least once during the 12 months preceding the certification signing date (3130(c)(2)), no later than the anniversary of the prior certification. | 3-6 years by record type per 17a-4. |
| **FINRA** | Rule 3130.04 | The certified report must be submitted to the board of directors / audit-committee no later than 45 days after the certification signing date (or the next scheduled meeting, whichever is earlier). | 3-6 years per 17a-4. |
| **FINRA** | Rule 3120 | Designated principals must test/verify Written Supervisory Procedures (WSPs) and submit a Rule 3120 Report to senior management no less than annually. FINRA recommends 3120 results feed the 3130 certification. | 3-6 years per 17a-4. |
| **SEC** | Rule 15c3-5 | BDs with market access must annually review pre-trade risk controls (15c3-5(d)(2)/(e)) and the CEO must separately certify that those controls comply. Top SEC exam priority for algo BDs. | 3-6 years per 17a-4. |
| **SEC** | Rule 17a-4(f) | Electronic recordkeeping must satisfy audit-trail attributes (see below). | BD records 3-6 years by type. |
| **SEC / FINRA Exams** | Quant Focus | Firms utilizing algorithmic strategies must specifically document risk controls, code integrity tests, and electronic trade surveillance. | Per underlying rule. |

## SEC Rule 17a-4(f) Electronic-Recordkeeping Audit-Trail Attributes

Since the 2023 amendment (SEC Release 34-96034, effective Jan 3, 2023; compliance May 3, 2023), BDs may preserve electronic records via EITHER:

1. **WORM (Write Once Read Many) storage**, OR
2. **An audit-trail alternative**: a complete time-stamped audit trail of all modifications/deletions able to recreate the original record, with a designated-executive-officer attestation option.

The audit-trail alternative requires the recordkeeping system to preserve: the original content, the time and date of any creation/modification/deletion, the identity of the actor performing the action, and the ability to recreate the original record as it existed at any point in time. Annual attestation evidence archived under either method satisfies 17a-4.

## SEC 206(4)-7 Annual Review Scope Caveat

The annual review is not a single date. SEC examiners scrutinize nine dimensions:

1. Who conducted the review
2. What was reviewed (scope)
3. When the review was conducted
4. How the review was conducted (methodology)
5. Findings identified
6. Recommendations made
7. Implementation status of prior recommendations
8. Documentation of the review
9. Senior-management sign-off

A review that "happened" but produced no documented findings and no senior-management sign-off should be treated as a deficiency/caveat by the CCO, even though the engine only models the completion/documentation date. The CCO must independently satisfy the nine dimensions; a passing engine verdict is necessary but not sufficient.

## FINRA Rule 4530 Self-Reporting

If a certification deadline is missed, FINRA Rule 4530(b) generally requires the firm to self-report the violation within 30 calendar days of discovery via FINRA Gateway. Late filings appear on the firm's 4530 Disclosure Timeliness Report Card. See `references/workflows.md` for the missed-attestation recovery path.
