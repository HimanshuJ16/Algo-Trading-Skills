# Workflows for New Strategy Onboarding Checklist

1. **Backtest Gate**:
   - Audit walk-forward score, regime coverage, and backtest Sharpe ratio.
2. **Operational Gate**:
   - Audit paper trading duration, execution errors, and kill switch integration.
3. **Model Risk & Compliance Gate**:
   - Audit model card documentation and compliance officer sign-off.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured onboarding audit report.
