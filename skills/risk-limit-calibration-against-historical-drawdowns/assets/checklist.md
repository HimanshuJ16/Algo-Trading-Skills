# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Is daily returns history $\ge 252$ trading days available for limit calibration?
- [ ] Are max drawdown depth %, duration days, VaR 99%, and CVaR 99% computed?
- [ ] Is a minimum 1.5x stress buffer multiplier applied to historical max drawdown?
- [ ] Is position sizing scalar reduced for strategies with historical drawdown > 20%?
