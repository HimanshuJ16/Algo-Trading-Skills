# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are counterparties and instrument issuers screened against OFAC SDN, EU, and UN lists?
- [ ] Is Levenshtein fuzzy name matching enabled with a threshold $\ge 85\%$?
- [ ] Is OFAC 50% Rule ownership percentage evaluated for unlisted subsidiaries?
- [ ] Are transactions with embargoed jurisdictions hard-blocked at pre-trade gate?
