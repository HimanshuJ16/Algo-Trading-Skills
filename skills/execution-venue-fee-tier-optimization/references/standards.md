# Standards for Execution Venue Fee Tier Optimization

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Net-Price Routing | SOR routing decisions MUST account for net exchange fees and maker rebates. |
| Minimum Fill Probability | Venues allocated volume MUST meet minimum fill probability thresholds ($\ge 0.80$). |
| Monthly Tier Audit | Volume allocations MUST be audited against monthly fee tier thresholds. |
