# Workflows for Algo Trading Entity KYC/AML Compliance

1. **UBO Look-Through Verification**:
   - Audit ownership structure down to natural persons holding $\ge 25\%$ equity/control.
2. **OFAC & PEP Sanctions Screening**:
   - Screen entity name and UBOs against global watchlist databases.
3. **FATF Jurisdiction Audit**:
   - Verify incorporation and banking jurisdictions against FATF blacklists.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured KYC/AML audit report.
