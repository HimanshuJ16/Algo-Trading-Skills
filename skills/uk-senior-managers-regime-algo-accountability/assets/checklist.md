# Institutional UK FCA SM&CR Governance Checklist

## Senior Management Function (SMF) & Responsibilities Map Setup
- [ ] **SMF Role Allocation**: Assign named SMF holders (SMF24 COO, SMF16 Compliance, SMF4 CRO) to every operational algorithmic trading strategy.
- [ ] **FCA Individual Reference Numbers (IRNs)**: Verify active FCA IRN credentials for all registered Senior Managers.
- [ ] **Statement of Responsibilities (SoR)**: Document exact algorithmic trading governance duties in each SMF holder's formal SoR.

## Certification Function Fitness & Propriety (F&P)
- [ ] **Developer F&P Background Checks**: Complete annual regulatory reference, criminal record, and financial soundness checks for all quant devs/traders.
- [ ] **SMF Accreditation Sign-Off**: Confirm all certified developers are accredited by a registered SMF holder.
- [ ] **Production Code Access Control**: Restrict production deployment privileges strictly to `FIT_AND_PROPER` certified individuals.

## Deployment Sign-Off & Reasonable Steps Audit
- [ ] **Pre-Trade Risk & Stress Test Approvals**: Verify written approval of RTS 6 pre-trade controls, stress testing, and Kill Switches.
- [ ] **Reasonable Steps Documentation**: Execute `execute_deployment_sign_off()` recording detailed audit notes on pre-deployment checks.
- [ ] **Annual MRM Audit Report**: Execute `generate_mrm_report()` and archive audit records for FCA statutory defense.
