# Workflows for Multi-Leg Strategy Margin Optimization

1. **Uncombined Margin Evaluation**:
   - Compute independent naked margin for all options legs.
2. **Combo Strategy Recognition**:
   - Identify multi-leg structures (Vertical Spreads, Iron Condors) and compute max defined risk margin.
3. **Savings & Efficiency Calculation**:
   - Compute capital savings and margin reduction percentages.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured margin optimization report.
