# Workflows for Smart Contract Audit Requirements Before DeFi Integration

1. **Audit Documentation Ingestion**:
   - Collect audit reports; verify firm reputation (Tier-1: Trail of Bits, OpenZeppelin, Consensys, Spearbit).
2. **Remediation & Fix Verification**:
   - Confirm all Critical/High findings are patched and verified by auditors.
3. **Governance & Timelock Audit**:
   - Verify $\ge 48$-hour timelock delay and $\ge 3$-of-$5$ multisig threshold.
4. **Integration Gate Verdict**:
   - Output integration approval decision and safety score %.
