# Standards for EU Benchmark Regulation (EU BMR)

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| ESMA Register Authorization | ALL financial benchmarks used by EU entities MUST be listed on the ESMA BMR Register. |
| Article 28(2) Fallback Plan | Strategies MUST maintain documented written plans for benchmark cessation. |
| Critical Benchmark Rule | Critical benchmarks (EURIBOR, ESTER) MUST have automated spread-adjusted fallback rate logic. |
