# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are in-flight capital caps ($\le 15\%$ NAV) configured for all active bridge protocols?
- [ ] Is real-time wrapped token de-peg monitoring ($> 1.0\%$) active?
- [ ] Are finality latency SLAs (minutes vs days) factored into cross-chain trade routing?
- [ ] Does bridge failover automatically select compliant secondary bridges upon de-peg alerts?
