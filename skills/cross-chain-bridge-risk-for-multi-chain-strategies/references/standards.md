# Standards for Cross-Chain Bridge Risk Management

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Single Bridge NAV Cap | No single cross-chain bridge MUST hold $> 15\%$ of total portfolio NAV in-flight simultaneously. |
| De-Peg Halt Threshold | Wrapped asset de-pegging $> 1.0\%$ MUST immediately halt new transfer routing to that bridge. |
| Smart Contract Audit Score | Bridges MUST possess at least 2 independent security audits with zero unresolved critical vulnerabilities. |
