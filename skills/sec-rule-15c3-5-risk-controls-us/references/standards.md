# Standards for SEC Rule 15c3-5 Risk Controls US

| Control | Standard Requirement |
|---|---|
| Direct & Exclusive Control | Risk controls MUST be operated under the direct and exclusive control of the broker-dealer. |
| Price Collar Cap | Orders deviating $> 5\%$ from NBBO mid MUST be rejected as fat-finger entries. |
| Reg SHO Short Locate | Short sale orders MUST verify a valid locate ID prior to order routing. |
