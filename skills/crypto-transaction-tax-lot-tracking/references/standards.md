# Standards for Crypto Transaction Tax Lot Tracking

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Taxable Swap Treatment | ALL Crypto-to-Crypto swaps MUST be treated as taxable dispositions at USD Fair Market Value. |
| Gas Fee Deductibility | Network gas fees MUST be subtracted from gross proceeds or added to cost basis. |
| HIFO Tax Optimization | HIFO tax lot selection MUST be enabled by default to minimize taxable gains. |
