# Standards for Leverage Limit Enforcement

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Max Gross Leverage | Global Gross Leverage MUST NOT exceed $3.0\times$ NAV. |
| Max Net Leverage | Global Net Directional Leverage MUST NOT exceed $1.5\times$ NAV. |
| Asset Class Caps | Crypto Perpetual Futures leverage MUST NOT exceed $3.0\times$. |
