# Workflows for Leverage Limit Enforcement

1. **Portfolio Exposure Calculation**:
   - Compute current gross and net notional exposures across active positions.
2. **Projected Leverage Impact Modeling**:
   - Model projected gross, net, and asset-class leverage including the proposed order.
3. **Pre-Trade Veto Audit**:
   - Audit projected leverage ratios against defined max caps and veto breaching orders.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured leverage enforcement report.
