# Standards: Benchmark Selection

| Metric | Target | Rationale |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| **Correlation to Benchmark** | > 0.7 for directional strategies | Shows the benchmark captures the core risk factors of the strategy. |
| **Information Ratio** | > 0.5 (good), > 1.0 (excellent) | Measures how much excess return is generated per unit of active risk (tracking error). |
| **Absolute Return Benchmark** | Cash / Risk-Free Rate | Market-neutral strategies should be benchmarked against a flat risk-free rate, not an equity index. |
