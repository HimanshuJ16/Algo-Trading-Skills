# Standards for Tax Lot Accounting Methods

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Long-Term Threshold | Holding period $> 365$ days MUST be classified as LTCG; $\le 365$ days as STCG. |
| Specific Identification | Specific lot designations MUST be recorded prior to settlement. |
| Lot Accounting Balance | Matched lot quantities MUST sum exactly to sale order quantity. |
