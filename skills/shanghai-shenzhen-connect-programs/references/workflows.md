# Workflows for Shanghai-Shenzhen Connect Programs

1. **Order Screening**:
   - Verify buy order quantity is a 100-share round lot multiple.
2. **T+1 Day Trading Audit**:
   - Ensure shares being sold were purchased prior to current trading day $T$.
3. **Daily Quota Verification**:
   - Deduct buy notional from RMB 52 billion daily quota balance per channel.
4. **Execution & Settlement**:
   - Settle securities on $T$ and cash on $T+1$ in CNH currency.
