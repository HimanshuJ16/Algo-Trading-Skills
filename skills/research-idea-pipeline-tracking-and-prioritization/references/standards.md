# Standards for Research Idea Pipeline Tracking and Prioritization

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Minimum Expected Sharpe | Ideas with expected Sharpe $< 1.2$ MUST NOT be prioritized for backtesting. |
| Minimum Capacity | Production candidate strategies MUST demonstrate $\ge \$5,000,000$ capacity. |
| Stage Review Cadence | Research pipeline review MUST occur bi-weekly to prune stalled backtests. |
