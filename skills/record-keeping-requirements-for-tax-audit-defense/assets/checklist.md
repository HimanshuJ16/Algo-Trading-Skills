# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are all trade records populated with mandatory fields (trade_id, symbol, side, qty, price, date, cost_basis)?
- [ ] Are holding periods classified as Short-Term ($\le 365$ days) or Long-Term ($> 365$ days)?
