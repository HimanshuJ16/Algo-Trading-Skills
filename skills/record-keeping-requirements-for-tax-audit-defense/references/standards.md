# Standards for Record-Keeping Requirements for Tax Audit Defense

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Record Retention | Trade records MUST be retained for a minimum of $7\text{ years}$. |
| Mandatory Fields | Every trade MUST include trade_id, symbol, side, quantity, price, trade_date, cost_basis_usd. |
| Holding Period | Short-Term $\le 365$ days; Long-Term $> 365$ days per IRS classification. |
