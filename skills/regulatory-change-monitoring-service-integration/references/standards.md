# Standards for Regulatory Change Monitoring Service Integration

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Urgent Action Window | Updates effective within $\le 30\text{ days}$ with CRITICAL/HIGH severity MUST trigger immediate action alerts. |
| Ingestion Frequency | Regulatory RSS/API feeds MUST be polled at least once every $24\text{ hours}$. |
| Audit Trail | All ingested regulatory updates and impact assessments MUST be logged permanently. |
