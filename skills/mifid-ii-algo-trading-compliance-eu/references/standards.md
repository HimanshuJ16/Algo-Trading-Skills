| Regulation / Standard | Technical Requirement |
|---|---|
| MiFID II / MiFIR (Directive 2014/65/EU) | Pre-trade risk controls, order tagging, and venue algorithmic trading obligations. |
| Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/589 (RTS 6) | Technical standards for organizational requirements of investment firms engaged in algorithmic trading. |
| RTS 6 Article 18 | Kill functionality requiring immediate halt of order flow and cancellation of open orders. |

Applies to all trading systems executing on EU regulated markets, Multilateral Trading Facilities (MTFs), and Organised Trading Facilities (OTFs).
