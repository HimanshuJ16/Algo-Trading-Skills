# Standards for Prime Brokerage Multi-Venue Consolidation

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Give-Up Submission Cutoff | Give-up trades MUST be submitted to PB before $T+0$ 18:00 EST. |
| Net Margin Efficiency | Consolidated net margin SHOULD reduce capital requirements by $\ge 30\%$. |
| Trade Idempotency | Execution IDs MUST be unique to prevent duplicate PB give-up entries. |
