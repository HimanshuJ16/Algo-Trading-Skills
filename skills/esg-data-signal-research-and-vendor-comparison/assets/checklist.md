# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are raw ESG scores from MSCI, Sustainalytics, and Refinitiv normalized to $[0.0, 1.0]$?
- [ ] Is multi-vendor consensus score calculated as a weighted or simple average?
- [ ] Is vendor disagreement dispersion ($\sigma_{\text{esg}}$) evaluated for greenwashing risk?
- [ ] Are controversial sector exclusion filters enforced prior to signal generation?
