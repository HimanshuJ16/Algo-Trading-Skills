# Workflows for Exchange for Physical (EFP) Transactions

1. **Trade Identification**:
   - Identify simultaneous futures and physical spot legs.
2. **Quantity Verification**:
   - Confirm physical asset quantity equals futures contract multiplier.
3. **Basis Evaluation**:
   - Calculate observed basis vs theoretical carrying cost forward price.
4. **Exchange Submission**:
   - Format EFRP reporting payload for CME/Eurex clearinghouse reporting.
