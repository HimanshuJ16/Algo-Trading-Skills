# Standards for Exchange for Physical (EFP) Transactions

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| CME Rule 538 Compliance | EFP trades MUST involve bona fide physical leg transfers without transitory wash features. |
| Quantity Equivalence | Physical leg quantity MUST match futures contract multiplier ($N_{\text{fut}} \times \text{Multiplier}$). |
| Reporting Window | EFRP trades MUST be reported to clearinghouses within 30 minutes of execution. |
