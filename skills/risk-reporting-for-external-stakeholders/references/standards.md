# Standards for Risk Reporting for External Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Report Cadence | Key Disclosed Metrics | Position Redaction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Limited Partner (LP) | Monthly / Quarterly | NAV, Gross/Net Leverage, VaR, Sharpe, Top 5 Sectors | Mandatory |
| Regulator (SEC/FCA) | Quarterly / Annual | Form PF / Annex IV, Full Sector & Liquidity Tiers | Mandatory |
| Prime Broker | Daily | Gross Exposure, Net Exposure, Liquidity Profile | Mandatory |
