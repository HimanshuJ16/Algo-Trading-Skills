# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are bilateral Double Taxation Treaties (DTT/DTAA) mapped for all entity-exchange pairs?
- [ ] Are active Tax Residency Certificates (TRC) or Form W-8BEN-E filings verified?
- [ ] Are WHT tax leakage savings quantified for cross-border dividend flows?
- [ ] Are Foreign Tax Credit (FTC) claims calculated to prevent double taxation?
