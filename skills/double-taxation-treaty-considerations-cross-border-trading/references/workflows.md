# Workflows for Double Taxation Treaty Considerations

1. **DTT Treaty Lookup**:
   - Match Residence Country and Source Country in treaty database.
2. **Withholding Tax Reduction Audit**:
   - Verify active Tax Residency Certificate (TRC) or Form W-8BEN-E.
3. **Tax Leakage & FTC Calculation**:
   - Compute WHT savings and Foreign Tax Credit claim ceiling.
4. **Compliance Documentation**:
   - Issue required tax forms and audit logs.
