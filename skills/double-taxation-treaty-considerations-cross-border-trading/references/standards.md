# Standards for Double Taxation Treaty Considerations

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| W-8BEN-E Renewal | Form W-8BEN-E / W-8BEN MUST be renewed every 3 years to maintain reduced DTT WHT rates. |
| DTT Beneficiary Status | Entities claiming treaty benefits MUST meet Beneficial Ownership criteria. |
| Foreign Tax Credit Cap | FTC claims MUST NOT exceed the lesser of foreign WHT paid or resident country tax liability. |
