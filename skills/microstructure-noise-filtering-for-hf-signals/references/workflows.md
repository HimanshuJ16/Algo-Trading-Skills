# Workflows for Microstructure Noise Filtering

1. **Raw Tick Stream Ingestion**:
   - Ingest tick data stream with prices and bid/ask volume depth.
2. **Noise Reduction Filtering**:
   - Apply Kalman Filter, Micro-Price, or EMA smoothing.
3. **Noise Variance & SNR Audit**:
   - Compute noise variance reduction percentage and SNR improvement.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured noise filtering report.
