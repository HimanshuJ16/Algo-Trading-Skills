# Standards for Job Posting Signal Analytics

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Ghost Listing Threshold | Postings open $> 120$ days MUST incur a $50\%$ stale haircut penalty. |
| Strategic Role Weight | Engineering/R&D postings MUST carry higher weight ($w = 1.5$) than general roles. |
| Score Range | Corporate Expansion Score MUST be normalized between $-1.0$ and $+1.0$. |
