# Workflows for Scenario-Based Stress Testing with Custom Shocks

1. **Portfolio Mapping**:
   - Map positions to risk factors and beta sensitivities.
2. **Scenario Shock Application**:
   - Apply historical crisis replays (2008, 2020, 2022) or custom factor shocks.
3. **Loss & Limit Breach Calculation**:
   - Calculate simulated P&L ($) and check against max drawdown limit %.
4. **Report Output & Capital Audit**:
   - Identify worst-case scenario loss and output stress test report.
