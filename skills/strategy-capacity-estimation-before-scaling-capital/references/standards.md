# Standards for Strategy Capacity Estimation Before Scaling Capital

| Parameter | Standard Limit | Description |
|---|---|---|
| Max ADV Participation | $\le 5.0\%$ of ADV | Prevents severe market impact and signaling risk. |
| Min Acceptable Sharpe | $\ge 1.0$ Net Sharpe | Minimum Sharpe threshold for institutional scaling. |
| Impact Formula | $\gamma \cdot \sigma \cdot \sqrt{\text{Size} / \text{ADV}}$ | Almgren-Chriss square-root impact law. |
