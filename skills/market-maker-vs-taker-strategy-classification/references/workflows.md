# Workflows for Strategy Classification

1. **Executed Trade Volume Audit**:
   - Decompose executed trade volume into Maker and Taker categories.
2. **Maker Ratio & Category Assignment**:
   - Calculate $R_{\text{maker}}$ and assign Pure Maker, Pure Taker, or Hybrid classification.
3. **Effective Fee Bps Audit**:
   - Calculate net fees paid vs gross notional and compute effective fee rate in bps.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured classification report.
