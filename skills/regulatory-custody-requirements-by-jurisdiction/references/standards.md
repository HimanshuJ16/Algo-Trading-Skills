# Standards for Regulatory Custody Requirements by Jurisdiction

| Jurisdiction | Regulator | Qualified Custodian | Segregation | Insurance Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US | SEC Rule 206(4)-2 | Mandatory | Mandatory | Optional |
| EU | ESMA MiCA / AIFMD | Mandatory | Mandatory | Mandatory (Digital Assets) |
| UK | FCA CASS | Mandatory | Mandatory | Optional |
| SG | MAS | Mandatory | Mandatory | Mandatory (Digital Assets) |
