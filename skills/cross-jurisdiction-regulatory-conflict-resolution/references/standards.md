# Standards for Cross-Jurisdiction Regulatory Conflict Resolution

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Rule Resolution Strategy | Cross-border trade orders MUST enforce the **Strictest Rule Primacy** across all overlapping regimes. |
| Zero Non-Compliant PFOF | PFOF MUST be blocked if ANY applicable jurisdiction prohibits order flow payments. |
| Legal Entity Identifier (LEI) | 20-character LEI MUST be present on all trade messages when required by any in-scope regime. |
