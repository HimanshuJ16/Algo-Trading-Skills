# Workflows for EU Short Selling Regulation (EU SSR)

1. **Locate & Borrow Verification**:
   - Audit locate agreement prior to short order entry.
2. **Net Short Position Calculation**:
   - Compute net short shares as percentage of issued share capital.
3. **Threshold Audit**:
   - Evaluate 0.1% private NCA notification and 0.5% public disclosure triggers.
4. **Regulatory Reporting**:
   - File SSR notifications by T+1 15:30 CET.
