# Standards for EU Short Selling Regulation (EU SSR)

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Private NCA Notification Threshold | Net short positions $\ge 0.1\%$ MUST be reported privately to NCAs by T+1 15:30 CET. |
| Public Disclosure Threshold | Net short positions $\ge 0.5\%$ MUST be publicly disclosed to the market by T+1 15:30 CET. |
| Naked Short Ban | Short equity orders MUST NOT be dispatched without valid locate / borrow confirmation. |
