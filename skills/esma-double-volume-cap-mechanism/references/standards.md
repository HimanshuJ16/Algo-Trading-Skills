# Standards for the MiFIR Article 5 Volume Cap Mechanism

Primary sources (all consulted 2026-08-24):

- **MiFIR Article 5 (volume cap mechanism)**, Regulation (EU) No 600/2014 as amended by Regulation (EU) 2024/791, via ESMA's Interactive Single Rulebook: https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mifir/article-5-volume-cap — Art. 5(1): *"Trading venues shall suspend their use of the waiver referred to in Article 4(1), point (a), where the percentage of trading in a financial instrument in the Union carried out under that waiver exceeds 7 % of the total volume of trading in that financial instrument in the Union."* Art. 5(4): ESMA publishes within seven working days of the end of March, June, September and December. Art. 5(8): the monitored/publication period starts **29 September 2025**. Art. 5(10): ESMA assesses the threshold annually from 29 September 2027, with the Commission able to adjust it by delegated act.
- **MiFIR Article 4(1) (waivers for equity instruments)**: https://www.esma.europa.eu/publications-and-data/interactive-single-rulebook/mifir/article-4-waivers-equity-instruments — (a) reference price systems; (b) negotiated transactions; (c) orders large in scale compared with normal market size; (d) orders held in an order management facility pending disclosure.
- **Regulation (EU) 2024/791 (MiFIR review)**, in force 28 March 2024: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ:L_202400791 — replaces the double volume cap with the single Union-wide cap.
- **ESMA, "[DISCONTINUED] Double Volume Cap Mechanism"**: https://www.esma.europa.eu/double-volume-cap-mechanism — *"the September 2025 publication will be the last under the Double Volume Cap"*; all DVC suspensions expire by 28 September 2025; *"From October 2025, the (Single) Volume Cap applies."*
- **ESMA, Volume Cap Mechanism**: https://www.esma.europa.eu/volume-cap-mechanism — quarterly results (January, April, July, October) at ISIN level; results in XML (SVCRES) on the ESMA register, suspension files in Excel; suspensions run three months and *"start at 8:00 am CET on the suspension start date and … end at the close of trading day on the suspension end date"*. October 2025 results published 09/10/2025, suspensions effective 14/10/2025–13/01/2026.
- **ESMA, "ESMA prepares for switch toward single volume cap in October 2025"**: https://www.esma.europa.eu/press-news/esma-news/esma-prepares-switch-toward-single-volume-cap-october-2025 — *"The new VCM limits at 7% the trading volume under the reference price waiver in the EU"*; venues *"suspend the use of the waiver for the concerned instrument for a period of three months"*; the DVCM reporting system is decommissioned in January 2026 and calculations move to NCA-collected transaction data.
- **RTS 1 — Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/587**, Annex II Tables 1 and 2 (orders large in scale compared with normal market size): https://ec.europa.eu/finance/securities/docs/isd/mifid/rts/160714-rts-1-annex_en.pdf · consolidated act: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2017/587/oj
- **Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/1246** (amends RTS 1/RTS 2; in force 23 November 2025, phased application to 2 March 2026 and 23 August 2026): https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg_del/2025/1246/oj/eng — the equity amendments located in this review touch Annex II Table 3 (standard market size) and add Table 3a; no amendment to the Annex II Table 1 LIS bands was identified. Treat the bands below as the statutory default and the ESMA per-ISIN transparency calculation as authoritative.

| Metric | Standard | Status | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cap threshold | 7 % of total Union trading volume in the instrument over the previous 12 months. | Mandatory, EU/EEA | MiFIR Art. 5(1) |
| Comparison | Suspension applies where the share **exceeds** the cap — strictly greater, on the unrounded share. Rounding to 2 dp before comparing turns 6.996 % into a suspension. | Mandatory (wording); engine requirement (unrounded) | MiFIR Art. 5(1) |
| Waivers in scope | Art. 4(1)(a) reference price waiver **only**. Art. 4(1)(b) negotiated, (c) large in scale and (d) order management facility are outside the cap. | Mandatory, EU/EEA | MiFIR Art. 5(1); ESMA VCM page |
| Authority for suspension | The venue suspends on the basis of **data published by ESMA**, within two working days of publication. A firm-computed ratio is monitoring, not status. | Mandatory, EU/EEA | MiFIR Art. 5(1) |
| Publication cadence | Quarterly — within seven working days of the end of March, June, September and December; results XML plus an Excel suspension file on the ESMA register. | Mandatory (ESMA obligation) | MiFIR Art. 5(4); ESMA VCM page |
| Suspension duration | Three months, inclusive of both endpoints (08:00 CET on the start date to the close of the trading day on the end date). | Mandatory, EU/EEA | MiFIR Art. 5(1); ESMA VCM page |
| Regime start | The monitored period begins 29 September 2025; first results file 9 October 2025, suspensions 14 October 2025 – 13 January 2026. | Effective date | MiFIR Art. 5(8); ESMA VCM page |
| Repealed DVC | 4 % per venue / 8 % Union-wide over 12 months, covering Art. 4(1)(a) and Art. 4(1)(b)(i), six-month suspension. Last publication September 2025; all suspensions expired 28 September 2025. **Backtest-only.** | Repealed | ESMA DVC page; pre-2024 MiFIR Art. 5 |
| Data source | From January 2026 the calculation is derived from transaction reports collected by NCAs; the venue-reported DVCM feed is decommissioned. Your own volume estimates will not reproduce it. | Operational | ESMA press release, October 2025 switch |
| LIS thresholds (shares / depositary receipts) | RTS 1 Annex II Table 1, by average daily turnover: <€50k → €15,000; €50k–100k → €30,000; €100k–500k → €60,000; €500k–1m → €100,000; €1m–5m → €200,000; €5m–25m → €300,000; €25m–50m → €400,000; €50m–100m → €500,000; ≥€100m → €650,000. **There is no flat threshold.** | Mandatory, EU/EEA | RTS 1 Annex II Table 1 |
| LIS thresholds (certificates) | RTS 1 Annex II Table 2: ADT < €50,000 → €15,000; ADT ≥ €50,000 → €30,000. | Mandatory, EU/EEA | RTS 1 Annex II Table 2 |
| LIS thresholds (ETFs / other equity-like) | Not set by Annex II Tables 1–2. Read the per-ISIN value from ESMA's published transparency calculations; the engine raises rather than deriving one. | Engine requirement | RTS 1 Annex II scope |
| Register staleness guard | A suspension file older than `max_register_age_days` (default 100) cannot cover the current quarter and is treated as unknown. An **engineering default sized from the quarterly cadence**, not a figure from the Regulation. | Engineering default | Engine requirement; MiFIR Art. 5(4) |
| Missing/stale register | Fail closed: block the capped waiver and route lit. Lit execution is always lawful; dark execution against an unknown suspension status is not. | Engineering default | Engine requirement |
| RPW numerator | The numerator is volume executed under the reference price waiver, not total dark volume. LIS and negotiated dark volume do not count toward the cap. | Mandatory (scope), engine invariant | MiFIR Art. 5(1) |
