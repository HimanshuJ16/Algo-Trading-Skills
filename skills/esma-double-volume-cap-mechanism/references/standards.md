# Standards for ESMA Double Volume Cap Mechanism

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Single Venue DVC Cap | Dark RPW trading volume MUST NOT exceed 4.0% of total EU market volume per venue. |
| EU-Wide DVC Cap | Combined dark RPW trading volume MUST NOT exceed 8.0% of total EU market volume. |
| LIS Waiver Exemption | Large-In-Scale (LIS) trades ($\ge €100,000$) ARE EXEMPT from DVC suspensions. |
