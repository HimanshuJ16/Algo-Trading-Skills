# Standards for Custody Solution Vendor Due Diligence

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| SEC Qualified Custodian | Custodians MUST hold SEC Qualified Custodian status (State/Federal Trust charter). |
| SOC 2 Type II | Custodians MUST provide an unqualified (clean) annual SOC 2 Type II audit report. |
| Bankruptcy Remoteness | Client assets MUST be strictly segregated and legally bankruptcy-remote. |
