# Workflows for Custody Solution Vendor Due Diligence

1. **Information Gathering**:
   - Collect SOC 2 Type II report, SEC/State Trust license, insurance binder, and BCP/DR plans.
2. **Pillar Evaluation & Scoring**:
   - Score Regulatory (25%), Security (25%), Insurance (20%), Operations (15%), and Governance (15%).
3. **Red Flag Audit**:
   - Intercept non-qualified status or co-mingling risks.
4. **Final Recommendation**:
   - Generate `APPROVED`, `CONDITIONAL_APPROVAL`, or `REJECTED` designation.
