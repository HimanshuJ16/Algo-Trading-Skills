# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are all orders explicitly tagged with capacity (`PROP` vs `CLIENT`)?
- [ ] Is FINRA Rule 5320 front-running detection active on same-side customer limit orders?
- [ ] Are Information Barriers (`info_barrier_id`) configured and verified between Prop and Agency desks?
- [ ] Is institutional customer opt-out documentation logged and verified for large orders ($\ge 10,000$ shares / $\$100,000$)?
