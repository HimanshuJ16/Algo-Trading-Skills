# Workflows for HK SFC Algorithmic Trading Compliance

1. **System & Developer Qualification**:
   - Audit developer registration and UAT sign-off.
2. **Pre-Trade Risk Audit**:
   - Audit order value, price deviation, and covered short selling locate checks.
3. **Emergency Kill Switch Integration**:
   - Provide instant kill switch to halt algos and cancel open orders.
4. **Audit Trail Logging**:
   - Log compliance decisions for 2-year SFC retention requirement.
