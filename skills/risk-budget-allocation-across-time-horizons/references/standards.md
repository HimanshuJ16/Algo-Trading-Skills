# Standards for Risk Budget Allocation Across Time Horizons

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Total Allocation | Sum of all horizon risk allocations MUST NOT exceed 100% of total portfolio risk budget. |
| Volatility Target | Each horizon MUST have an explicit annualized vol target calibrated to its holding period. |
| Drawdown Limit | Each horizon MUST enforce an independent max drawdown limit. |
