# Workflows for Illiquid Auction Execution

1. **Pre-Trade ADV Assessment**: Before executing any parent order, the execution system queries the 30-day Average Daily Volume (ADV) for the target instrument. ADV may be fractional; `total_qty` may not — the engine rejects a non-integer share count rather than emitting fractional child orders.
2. **Session calendar lookup**: Resolve the **scheduled** end of Core Trading Hours for the trading date before anything else. Every deadline in step 5 is derived from it, and it is 1:00 p.m. ET on early-close days (see `global-exchange-holiday-calendar-handling`). Pass it as `market_close_et`; `REGULAR_SESSION_CLOSE_ET` (16:00) and `EARLY_CLOSE_SESSION_CLOSE_ET` (13:00) are provided.
3. **Routing Decision**: Pass `total_qty` and `average_daily_volume` to `IlliquidAuctionExecutionEngine.generate_routing_plan()`. Supply `reference_price`, `slippage_tolerance_bps`, `side` ("BUY"/"SELL") and `tick_size` so the engine can populate a submittable `suggested_limit_price`. The engine validates that `total_qty` is a positive integer, `average_daily_volume` is a finite positive number, `symbol` is non-empty, `side` is BUY/SELL, and `tick_size` is finite and positive.
4. **Limit-Price Hydration**: If `suggested_limit_price` is `None` (no reference price was supplied) but `auction_qty > 0` with `auction_order_type == LIMIT_ON_CLOSE`, the caller MUST assign a limit price before submission. An LOC order without a limit price will be rejected by the exchange (NYSE Rule 7.31(c)(2)(A)). A hydrated price must itself be a whole multiple of the instrument's minimum price variation — a sub-penny limit on a stock at or above $1.00 breaches SEC Rule 612 and is rejected.
5. **Cutoff Enforcement**: Before routing, call `validate_submission_window(submission_time, market_close_et=<scheduled close>)` with a timezone-aware datetime in **any** zone; the module converts it to `America/New_York` before comparing, and rejects naive datetimes. The default cutoff is ten minutes before the scheduled close (15:50 on a regular day, 12:50 on a 1:00 p.m. half day). For a venue-specific deadline pass `cutoff=entry_cutoff_for(venue, order_type, market_close_et)`: NYSE 15:50 for both MOC and LOC, Nasdaq 15:55 for MOC and 15:58 for LOC on a regular close.
6. **Late-window qualification**: If the submission falls between the conservative cutoff and the venue-specific one, verify the extra condition the rules impose before relying on it. NYSE accepts a post-freeze MOC/LOC only when it is contra to a *published* Significant Closing Imbalance and rejects everything else; a Nasdaq LOC entered in its final three minutes requires a First or Second Reference Price and is rejected or re-priced when more aggressive than it. If you are not reading the imbalance feed, stop at the conservative cutoff.
7. **Continuous Slicing**: If `continuous_qty > 0`, route that portion to a standard VWAP or TWAP execution algorithm scheduled to finish at least five minutes before the entry cutoff — 3:45 p.m. on a regular close, 12:45 p.m. on a 1:00 p.m. half day — so the auction leg can still be sized and submitted in time.
8. **Auction Submission**: If `auction_qty > 0`, submit a Limit-on-Close (LOC) order with the (hydrated or computed) limit price prior to the cutoff. MOC must NOT be used for illiquid names.
9. **Post-freeze commitment**: After `cancel_modify_freeze_for(market_close_et)` the auction leg cannot be freely cancelled or reduced on either venue — NYSE not even for a legitimate error (sole exception: Rule 7.35B(j)(2)(B)), Nasdaq only for a legitimate error and only until three minutes before the close. Treat resting auction quantity as committed to an unknown closing price and size it accordingly.
10. **Post-cross reconciliation**: Compare fills against the Official Closing Price and attribute unfilled LOC quantity as opportunity cost. An LOC that did not fill because the cross printed through its limit is the price protection working, not a routing failure.
