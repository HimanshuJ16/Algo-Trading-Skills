# Standards for Single-Name Concentration Risk

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Single-Name Hard Limit | No single stock position MUST exceed 5% of portfolio NAV (unless specifically mandated by a concentrated strategy mandate). |
| Liquidity Limit | Order quantity MUST NOT exceed 10% of 20-day Average Daily Volume (ADV) to avoid execution market impact. |
| Pre-Trade Enforcement | Concentration checks MUST be executed synchronously before submitting order messages to brokers/exchanges. |
