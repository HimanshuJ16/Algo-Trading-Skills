# Pre-Flight Checklist

- [ ] Are single-name NAV concentration limits (e.g. 5% NAV) configured and tested?
- [ ] Are Average Daily Volume (ADV) liquidity limits (e.g. 10% ADV) integrated into pre-trade order sizing?
- [ ] Does the limiter automatically downsize oversized orders to the maximum compliant share count?
- [ ] Is Herfindahl-Hirschman Index (HHI) and Effective Assets ($N_{eff}$) computed for portfolio risk reporting?
