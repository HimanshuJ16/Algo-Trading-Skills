# Standards for 1099-B and Broker Tax Reporting Reconciliation

| Standard / Term | Description |
|---|---|
| **IRS Form 1099-B** | Proceeds from Broker and Barter Exchange Transactions. The official document reported to the IRS. |
| **IRS Form 8949** | Sales and Other Dispositions of Capital Assets. Used by the taxpayer to report capital gains/losses. |
| **Trade Date Accounting** | IRS requirement that trades be reported based on the trade execution date, not the settlement date. |
| **Section 1256 Contracts** | Special tax treatment for regulated futures contracts and broad-based index options (60% long-term / 40% short-term). |
| **Wash Sale (Section 1091)**| Disallowance of a loss if a "substantially identical" security is purchased 30 days before or after the sale. |
