# Standards for IRS Risk Management

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Pay-Fixed DV01 Sign | Pay-Fixed swaps MUST carry positive DV01 (short duration / gains on rate rise). |
| Receive-Fixed DV01 Sign | Receive-Fixed swaps MUST carry negative DV01 (long duration / gains on rate drop). |
| DV01 Neutrality Hedge | Required hedge notional MUST be calculated to bring net portfolio DV01 to 0. |
