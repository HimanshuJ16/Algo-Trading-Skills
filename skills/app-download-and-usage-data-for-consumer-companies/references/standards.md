# Standards for Consumer App Data Signals

| Metric | Definition | Threshold |
|---|---|---|
| **Stickiness Ratio** | DAU / MAU | >50% is world-class. ~20% is average. |
| **Leaky Bucket Syndrome** | High Downloads + Low Stickiness | Signals aggressive but unprofitable customer acquisition cost (CAC) spend. |
| **Vanity Metrics** | Cumulative Downloads | Ignoring this metric; it has near-zero correlation with long-term enterprise value. |
