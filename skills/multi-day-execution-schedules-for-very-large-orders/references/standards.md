# Standards for Multi-Day Execution Schedules

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Participation Cap | Daily participation MUST NOT exceed $15\%$ of ADV (default $10\%$). |
| Minimum Horizon | Execution horizon MUST be $\ge \lceil Q_{\text{total}} / (p_{\text{max}} \cdot \text{ADV}) \rceil$. |
| Overnight Risk Audit | Overnight volatility risk MUST be quantified for remaining unexecuted inventory. |
