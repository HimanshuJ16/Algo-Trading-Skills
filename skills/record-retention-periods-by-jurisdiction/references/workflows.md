# Workflows for Record Retention Periods by Jurisdiction

1. **Jurisdiction Lookup**:
   - Map record jurisdiction code to minimum regulatory retention period.
2. **Retention Compliance Validation**:
   - Compare current retention against jurisdictional minimum; flag shortfalls.
3. **Expiry Risk Calculation**:
   - Calculate years remaining to meet minimum retention.
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured retention compliance report.
