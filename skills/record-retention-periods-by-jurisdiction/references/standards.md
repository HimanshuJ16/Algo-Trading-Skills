# Standards for Record Retention Periods by Jurisdiction

| Jurisdiction | Regulator | Min Retention |
|---|---|---|
| US | SEC (Rule 17a-4) | 7 years |
| UK | FCA (SYSC 9) | 5 years |
| Singapore | MAS | 5 years |
| Australia | ASIC | 7 years |
| India | SEBI | 8 years |
| EU | MiFID II | 5 years |
