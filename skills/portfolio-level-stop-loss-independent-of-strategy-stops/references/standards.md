# Standards for Portfolio-Level Stop-Loss Independent of Strategy Stops

| Metric | Engineering Standard |
|---|---|
| Max Daily Drawdown Standard | Default limit MUST NOT exceed $5.0\%$ of start-of-day equity. |
| Max Peak Drawdown Standard | Default limit MUST NOT exceed $10.0\%$ of historical peak equity. |
| Post-Breach Action | Emergency global market order flatten + trading lockout. |
