# Workflows for Post-Trade Execution Quality Scorecard

1. **Implementation Shortfall & VWAP Calculation**:
   - Compute IS (bps) and VWAP slippage (bps) per executed order.
2. **Effective Spread & EQR Calculation**:
   - Calculate Effective Spread and EQR ratio against arrival midquote/quoted spread.
3. **Fill Rate & Composite Grading**:
   - Compute Fill Rate % and calculate composite scorecard grade (A/B/C/D/F).
4. **Audit Report Generation**:
   - Output structured execution quality scorecard report.
